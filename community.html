In the community

Coaching, sponsoring and showing up for Walnut Creek, Danville and the water polo community since 1998.

Dr. Johnson has coached Little League, CYO basketball and youth flag football, served on the Walnut Heights swim club board, and sponsored Las Lomas, Acalanes and San Ramon Valley High School athletics and USA Water Polo for more than a decade. He has hosted free athlete screening events, run a race-therapy tent at the Walnut Creek Turkey Trot, and brought TRUtalks and injury-prevention talks to athletes, parents and coaches.

The full list of coaching roles, sponsorships, charities and events, with the public record behind each, is on his CV.
