Clinical research

Eight pilot-scale studies from the TRUSALUS screening and treatment data, 2018 to 2024.

From 2018 to 2024 the practice collected baseline, screening and treatment data on its athletes and patients, with consent, and in 2026 wrote the results up as eight pilot-scale studies. They are observational, in-practice and de-identified. They are offered as what one clinic saw, not as trials.

  • Principal investigator, TRUSALUS clinical research program, 2018 to 2024: eight observational, pilot-scale studies of injury burden, movement deficits and lifestyle risk in youth, collegiate and adult recreational athletes (aggregate, de-identified data; manuscripts prepared October 2026, not yet submitted)
  • Study 1. The 2018 TRUathletes launch: building a youth athletic baseline (39 athlete profiles, 40 baseline tests), 2018
  • Study 2. High school team injury and lifestyle screening pilot, Las Lomas football and Monte Vista water polo (44 athletes), 2018 to 2019
  • Study 3. Community lifestyle and injury questionnaire: energy availability and sleep associations (203 individuals), 2018 to 2020
  • Study 4. The functional movement screen cohort: deficits, asymmetry and re-test outcomes (508 individuals, more than 1,100 screens), 2019 to 2021
  • Study 5. The injury tracker: injury location, onset, functional impact and overtraining symptoms (106 individuals), 2019 to 2021
  • Study 6. The holistic injury recovery plan: an individualized prescription engine (219 individuals), 2020 to 2021
  • Study 7. The TRUathletes Health Score: a composite scoring instrument in adult recreational athletes (133 individuals), 2022
  • Study 8. Female athlete energy-availability screening, Mustang Soccer Girls ECNL and Saint Mary's women's tennis (about 92 screened, 14 enrolled; 153 collegiate entries), 2023 to 2024
  • Overview manuscript in preparation: "Functional Movement Deficits, Lifestyle Risk Factors, and Injury Burden in a Community Chiropractic and Athletic Population," prepared for the Journal of Chiropractic Medicine, October 2026
  • Poster abstract and oral-session proposal prepared for the 2027 Female Athlete Conference, October 2026
  • Screening instruments: LEAF-Q, EAT-26, Eating Attitude and Energy Balance questionnaires, body-composition testing, functional movement screen, TRUathletes Health Score
