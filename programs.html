Programs

Twenty years of programs for athletes, teams and the community, and what is on offer now.

Programs Dr. Johnson built, from Danville's 28 Days to Health to the TRUathletes platform and today's TRU Holistic Injury Recovery.

Today

  • TRU Holistic Injury Recovery (2024 to present): the three-step plan, restore, re-educate, retrain, with chiropractic, NMR, corrective exercise and SoftWave.
  • Low Energy Availability and energy-balance screening for female athletes: lecture, questionnaires (LEAF-Q, EAT-26), body-composition testing and a 12-week individualized plan, delivered to Mustang Soccer Club Girls ECNL (2023 to 2024) and Saint Mary's College women's tennis (2024).
  • Team injury screens: functional-movement screening with a team report for the coach.
